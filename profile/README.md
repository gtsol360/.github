## 🏢 Who We Are

**GTSol360** (Global Technology Solution 360) is a **founder-led technology agency** headquartered in **Karachi, Pakistan**. Since **2019**, we've partnered with startups, scale-ups, and enterprises to **design, build, and ship software that moves real business metrics**.

We're not a body-shop. We don't do templates. Every product we ship is **custom-built, production-grade, and owned end-to-end** by the same founders who set your strategy.

> ### 💬 *"Founder-led means zero handoffs. The people who scope your project are the ones signing off on delivery."*
>
> — **Abrar Ahmed**, Founder
